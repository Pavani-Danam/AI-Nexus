AI-Nexus Document Ingestion & RAG Architecture.
Section 1: Asynchronous Processing Pipeline.
The pipeline extracts text from uploaded documents, creates semantic chunks with configurable overlap, and estimates token lengths.

Section 2: Vector Embedding & Search.
Each document chunk will be vectorized into high-dimensional embeddings and indexed in a vector store for fast similarity retrieval.

Section 3: Contextual Augmentation.
The retrieved chunks are augmented into LLM prompts for grounded response generation.
